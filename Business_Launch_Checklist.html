Business Launch Checklist

Fill this in, then save a copy for your records.

Everything you need before launching your business

📋 Strategy & Vision

  • Define your business idea clearly (what problem does it solve?)
  • Identify your target customer (who specifically?)
  • Validate market demand (talk to 10+ potential customers)
  • Analyze competitors (who else solves this problem?)
  • Define your unique value proposition (why choose you?)
  • Set clear business goals for year 1

🎨 Brand & Identity

  • Choose your business name
  • Create/refine your logo
  • Define your brand colors and fonts
  • Write your brand story (why you started this)
  • Develop your brand voice/messaging
  • Design brand guidelines document
  • Create social media profiles (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook)

💰 Offer & Pricing

  • Define your product/service in detail
  • Calculate your unit economics (cost + profit margin)
  • Research competitor pricing
  • Decide on your pricing strategy
  • Plan your pricing tiers/packages (if applicable)
  • Determine payment methods you'll accept

📈 Marketing & Growth

  • Identify your top 3 marketing channels
  • Create your website/landing page
  • Plan your content strategy (blog, social, email)
  • Build an email list (create lead magnet)
  • Set up email marketing platform
  • Plan your launch announcement strategy
  • Identify potential partnership/affiliate opportunities

⚙️ Operations & Finance

  • Choose your business structure (LLC, sole prop, etc.)
  • Register your business name
  • Get required licenses/permits
  • Open a business bank account
  • Set up basic accounting/bookkeeping system
  • Create financial projections (6-12 months)
  • Determine startup costs and funding needs
  • Set up invoicing system

🚀 Launch Readiness

  • Document your standard operating procedures
  • Set up customer service process (email, chat, phone)
  • Prepare FAQ for common customer questions
  • Plan your soft launch (beta customers/friends)
  • Create launch timeline (what happens when?)
  • Brief any team members/partners on launch plan
  • Write and schedule launch announcements
  • Prepare success metrics (what will you measure?)

Feeling overwhelmed? That's normal. Most first-time founders miss key steps.
